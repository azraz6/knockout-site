Knockout is designed around a simple idea: a sale is an instant, and your preparation should be built around that instant — not around a timer that accumulates drift.

01 / TIME
⌁

Reference-clock calibration

Knockout measures an SNTP reference through Apple’s time service, rejects outliers and shows the actual offset, round trip and uncertainty. It is never presented as Shopee server time.

02 / PREPARE
◷

Offsets and local reminders

Set a preparation offset from −2.5 seconds to zero. Schedule preparation and open-app reminders on your iPhone. No account and no background process required.

03 / FOCUS
◉

A countdown you can read

A high-contrast instrument with milliseconds while foregrounded, haptic feedback and a Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activity driven by the system clock.
