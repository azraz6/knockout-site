Getting started

Knockout works without an account. Create a sale event in Events, add preparation reminders, then open the Timer tab to arm the precision countdown. Tap “Clock details” to see the reference calibration. You can also start a quick timer or a custom target without an event.

Why does the app show “device time”?

Knockout only claims a synchronized reference when it has a fresh, measured calibration. If the calibration is stale, unavailable or the device clock changed, the app falls back to your iPhone clock and says so. Countdowns continue to work offline. Tap “Sync now” in Clock details when you have a connection.

Is this Shopee server time?

No. Knockout measures a general-purpose time reference (SNTP via Apple’s time service with fallbacks) and shows the measured uncertainty. It is not Shopee server time, is not affiliated with Shopee, and cannot guarantee checkout acceptance. Sale times are the times you enter.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. If notifications were denied, enable them in iOS Settings → Notifications → Knockout, then return to Knockout so reminders can be rescheduled. The Settings screen shows how many reminders are currently scheduled. A “Preview notification” action is available for testing.

Live Activities and Dynamic Island

Live Activities must be enabled in iOS Settings → Knockout. Open an event and tap “Lock Screen,” or use “Show on Lock Screen” in the Timer tab. The countdown is rendered by iOS and is not updated millisecond-by-millisecond in the background. Stop the activity from the same button. Availability depends on your device and iOS version.

Restoring Knockout Plus

Open Settings or the paywall and choose Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account. If nothing restores, confirm that you are signed in with the purchasing Apple Account and that the purchase is active in your Apple Account subscriptions. Refunds and billing are handled by Apple.

Managing a subscription

Use Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or the Manage subscription link in the app. Knockout cannot cancel or refund an App Store purchase directly.

Why is there an ad on Home or Events?

The free version is supported by non-personalised ads on Home, Events and timing history. Ads never appear on the active timer or reaction trainer. Knockout Plus removes them. See Privacy for data details.

Wishlist links

Only http and https links are accepted. A link opens only when you tap the arrow or the open action. If the marketplace app is installed, the system may hand the link to it; otherwise it opens in your browser. Knockout does not fetch prices or sign in to marketplaces.

Timer accuracy

The foreground timer uses a monotonic clock and recomputes target minus current time every frame. The displayed reference status and uncertainty are measured, not decorative. For the most reliable measurement, keep the app foregrounded, use a fresh calibration, and avoid changing the device clock.

Privacy

Sale events, wishlist items, photos, reaction results and settings stay on your device. There is no Knockout account and no Knockout analytics service. Read the full privacy policy.
