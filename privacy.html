Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Knockout is a local-first sale timing and preparation utility. This policy explains what the app stores, the limited network requests it makes, and the choices you have. The data controller is the Knockout developer, contactable at info@qshoppro.com.

Short version
We do not require a Knockout account. Your events, reminders, wishlist, photos and reaction results stay on your iPhone. We do not operate a Knockout backend that receives your shopping content. Clock calibration contacts general-purpose time services without sending your shopping data. The free version uses non-personalised Google Mobile Ads.

1. Data stored on your device

Knockout stores the following locally in its app container:

  • Sale event names, notes, marketplaces, dates, time zones and repeat settings.
  • Reminders and notification preferences.
  • Wishlist product names, optional links, target prices, notes and optional photos.
  • Clock calibration measurements and diagnostic history.
  • Timer sessions and reaction-training results.
  • Display, theme, calibration, sound and haptics preferences.
  • A local mirror of your StoreKit entitlement status.

This data is not sent to a Knockout server. It remains until you delete it in the app, delete the app, or restore a device backup. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

2. Clock calibration

When you synchronise the clock, Knockout contacts general-purpose time services:

  • time.apple.com over SNTP/UDP as the primary reference.
  • time.cloudflare.com over SNTP/UDP as a fallback.
  • https://www.apple.com HTTP date headers as a coarse fallback.

These providers receive ordinary network metadata such as your IP address, request timing and routing information. Knockout does not send your events, wishlist, account information or credentials. The reference is an estimate and is not Shopee server time. Each provider’s own privacy policy applies to its processing.

3. Notifications

Sale reminders are scheduled locally through Apple’s UserNotifications framework. Reminder content is generated on your device. Notification permission is optional, and you can change it in iOS Settings. Denying permission does not stop the timer from working.

4. Purchases

Knockout Plus — monthly, annual and lifetime — is purchased through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payment, billing, renewals and subscription management. Knockout receives entitlement status, not your card or payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

5. Advertising

The free version displays advertising through Google Mobile Ads on Home, the Events list and timing history only. Knockout requests non-personalised advertising and does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt. Google and its partners may process device information, approximate network location, ad interactions and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads under their policies. The app does not receive your payment card or marketplace credentials from ads.

Knockout Plus removes advertising. See Google’s privacy policy and advertising technologies.

6. Marketplace links

Tapping a product or sale link opens the relevant marketplace app or your browser. Those services have their own privacy policies. Knockout does not sign in to a marketplace, scrape authenticated content or receive marketplace account credentials. It does not automate purchases or checkout.

7. Analytics and diagnostics

There is no third-party analytics SDK in Knockout. We do not send usage analytics or crash analytics to a Knockout server. Apple may provide the developer with aggregated App Store analytics and crash reports under Apple’s terms. Local timing diagnostics stay on your device until you delete them or delete the app.

8. Legal bases (where required)

  • Requested service: local app functionality, clock calibration you initiate, and purchases.
  • Consent: notifications and any optional advertising consent. You can withdraw consent in settings.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the app secure, preventing abuse and supporting core functionality.
  • Legal obligations: tax, accounting, app-store and consumer obligations.

9. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete individual items, clear results, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes local app data; device backups may retain copies. Apple retains purchase records under its policies, and ad providers retain data under their policies.

You can delete events, reminders, products and timing history in the app, clear reaction results, use “Use device time” to clear the applied calibration, revoke notification permission in iOS Settings, and manage subscriptions in your Apple Account. To contact us about data, email info@qshoppro.com.

10. Security

Knockout relies on iOS platform protections and keeps sale and wishlist content local. No method of storage is completely secure. Protect your device with a passcode and keep iOS current. The only network requests made by the app are the limited calibration requests described above, plus advertising and StoreKit traffic when those features are used.

11. Children

Knockout is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

12. International processing

Time services, Apple and Google may process data in countries other than yours under their safeguards and policies. The app itself does not transfer your sale or wishlist content to us internationally.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, port or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because most data is local, many of these choices can be exercised directly in the app. Contact info@qshoppro.com for requests about data the developer controls. You may also contact your local data protection authority, such as the Department of Personal Data Protection in Malaysia.

14. Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The “Last updated” date above will change, and material changes will be reflected in the app or release notes. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies.

15. Independence

Knockout is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop or Zalora. Marketplace names are used only to label user-created content. Product links are not affiliate links by default; if affiliate links are added after programme approval, this page and the app will disclose that relationship.
